Research Focus

My research investigates the interface of Scientific Machine Learning (SciML), Symplectic Mechanics, Inverse Problems, and High-Dimensional Stochastic PDEs. Rather than treating neural networks as empirical black boxes, my work formulates neural architectures around exact mathematical invariants:

  • Geometric Structure Preservation: Preserving exact symplectic differential 2-forms ($\omega = \sum dq^i \wedge dp_i$) and contact Hamiltonian phase spaces for non-conservative and relativistic celestial orbits.
  • The Adaptive Subspace (AS) Paradigm: Resolving capacity interference and destructive multi-task gradient conflict via parameter null-space decomposition ($\Theta_0 \oplus \Theta_1$) and parameter-space Adaptive Mesh Refinement (AMR).
  • Curse of Dimensionality Elimination: Forward-mode directional autograd trace contractions evaluating high-dimensional diffusion operators in strictly $\mathcal{O}(d)$ linear complexity ($<3\,\mathrm{GB}$ VRAM for 50-asset American basket options).
  • Zero-Allocation Real-Time Systems: Deterministic 2000Hz soft-body physics telemetry engines and high-performance finite difference solvers operating at hardware memory bandwidth ceilings.
